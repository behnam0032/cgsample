// ABOUT ARTAKAN

Tools built by people who model architecture too

Artakan is a small studio that builds MaxScript plugins for architectural visualization — the kind of repetitive modeling work every archviz artist and architect runs into on nearly every project: stairs, floor plans, and area takeoffs.

TIMELINE
2012

Create Stairs, first release

Published on ScriptSpot and sold through CGSample.com — our first parametric plugin for 3ds Max.

2016

Architect Desktop

A follow-up toolkit built for the measurement and takeoff work architects do every day.

2025

PlanTo3D

Our newest plugin, turning 2D floor plans into full 3D building models.

2026
