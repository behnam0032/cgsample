Create Stairs

A fully parametric stair, railing and handrail system for 3ds Max. Set your parameters once, then just draw the run of the staircase — the plugin builds the rest.

Buy — email us ScriptSpot page
SPECIFICATIONS

What it builds

Instead of separate stair and railing objects, the plugin generates one parametric system where every part stays editable.

01

Parametric stairs

Set rise, tread depth, width and type (straight, turned, double entry) with a few simple spinners.

02

Railing & handrail

A metal railing with vertical balusters and a wooden handrail is generated automatically along the stair path.

03

Material control

Separate material slots for treads, railing and handrail — works with V-Ray and Corona.

04

Live editing

Change the stair's length or angle at any time without losing materials or previous settings.

05

Lightweight output

Optimized mesh with a low poly count, suited to heavy architectural scenes.

06

Version compatibility

Tested on common 3ds Max versions; email us before purchase to confirm your version.
