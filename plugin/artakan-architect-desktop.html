SPECIFICATIONS

What it calculates

Architect Desktop reads geometry directly from your scene, so figures update as the design changes.

01

Room perimeter

Select a room or hall and get its perimeter instantly, for skirting and finishing takeoffs.

02

Façade area

Measure the area of a façade, including openings, straight from the model.

03

Glazing area

Total glass area across selected windows, useful for energy and cost estimates.

04

Floor area

Net floor area per room or across the whole plan, for client-facing area schedules.

05

Export-ready figures

Results are formatted for quick copy into reports or client proposals.

06

Built for offices

Made to speed up the quantity takeoffs architects do on every project.
