ABOUT / CGSAMPLE

Software for Architects and 3D Artists

CGSample develops digital tools for architectural visualization and 3D modeling. The products are focused on practical problems that architects, designers and 3D artists encounter during everyday work.

From parametric stair generation to architectural calculations and floor-plan modeling, the goal is to turn repetitive operations into faster and more manageable workflows inside Autodesk 3ds Max.

CGSample products are developed with a focus on usability, speed and practical architectural workflows.

QUESTIONS / ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CGSample?

CGSample is a software and digital tools brand focused on Autodesk 3ds Max plugins and architectural workflows for architects, designers and 3D artists.

What is Artakan Create Stairs?

Artakan Create Stairs is a parametric staircase plugin for Autodesk 3ds Max that helps users create stairs, railings and handrails with adjustable parameters.

What does PlanTo3D do?

PlanTo3D is designed to convert 2D architectural floor plans into 3D building elements such as walls, openings and floors inside Autodesk 3ds Max.

Who are CGSample plugins for?

CGSample plugins are designed primarily for architects, interior designers, architectural visualization artists, 3D modelers and other Autodesk 3ds Max users.
